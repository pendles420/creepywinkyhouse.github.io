The Most Amazingly Beautiful Part of Waking Up Is When You Put Some Coffee Into The Inside of Your Cup
by marta sharapova

The rectangular conference room recirculated its circular air. Outside, Manhattan was so sweltering hot that there were welts developing, while inside, the men from the coffee company sat in a very polite row in their brown suits, brown shoes, brown hair, beautiful brown eyes, and brown ambitions while a magnified beam of sunlight roasted them like beans. At the head of the table stood the ever-tailored and self possessed Don Draper, steeped in revelation.

The blinds were drawn and a small projector played the ad demo:

“Good morning world!” said the alarm clock.

“Top of the day!” sang the kettle.

“There’s still time,” ticked the clock.

“I tried my best,” flickered the bedside lamp.

“You forgot me,” wilted the bouquet, bowing its head.

The slogan slowly fades into view; “Forgive the morning, it’s still brewing.”

The reel clicked away and the blinds opened up to a gaping silence.

One of the executives cleared his throat. “Our product contains caffeine, Mr. Draper. Not… absolution.”

Draper nodded curtly and strained a neutered smile under the watchful gaze of Duck Philips as the men poured out of the room like coffee.

O N E   M O N T H   L A T E R

A girl sits pensively with a mug~ her legs covered with a blanket, she’s looking away, clutching the cup, holding it in her hands, cupping it tightly with her fingers, and rolling it into her palms. A small steam rises. The girl smiles about her memories and takes a sip.

“CUT!” shouted the director, “Camera, camera, camera!”

“Yes?” said the girl, because that was her name.

“I just… don’t believe you, you know what I mean? I want to believe you here.”

“No, I swear it’s my name! If you want you can call me something else—”

“I have an idea!” interrupted a little man. “What about, like, a coffee-colored horse and it’s saying ‘this is my last straw!’ but it’s actually hay!” He collapsed laughing.

Suddenly, an iPhone with a catchy ringtone started going off:

🎵 You can have my honey heart! My honey heart! 🎶

As it faded out of its initial ringtone cycle, the director became noticeably meerkat-like.

“What was that song?!” asked the director.

Camera darted over to turn off her cell, profusely blushing.

“It’s nothing,” she stammered. “Just something I played around with in my free time to get ready for the ad.”

“Bold move making it your ringtone…” the director said. He was impressed.

“Perform the whole song for us! Perform the whole song for us!” chanted the crew.

Camera playfully sauntered over to the soundstage and began to sing the following song so breathlessly and at such a rapid tempo that the words practically whizzed forth:

🎶

“You can have my honey heart, my honey heart,
Golden sunlight makes it start.
Coffee blooming rich and dark,
Butter melting, meadow-spark.
You can have my honey heart, my honey heart,
Curtains drifting soft apart.
Cinnamon and rainbows’ arc,
Morning dew so sweet and tart.
You can have my honey heart, my honey heart,
Sparrows contrast light and dark.
Maple syrup apple cart,
Morning school be really smart
You can have my honey heart, my honey heart,
Apricot and amber tart.
Toasted hazelnut bright spark,
Sap is dripping from the bark.
You can have my honey heart, my honey heart,
Porcelain and teaspoon parts.
Laundry dancing in the yard.
Orange blossoms leave kiss marks,
You can have my honey heart, my honey heart!”

In the back of the room, a pair of eyes desperately brimmed with salty, bitter tears as Don Draper whispered, “Forgive the morning.”

Camera caught the gleam and did a close up on him.

“I’m trying,” she said.
